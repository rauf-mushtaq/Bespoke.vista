Security and data protection

How we protect your operations and data.

Where we process personal data for you, you are normally the controller and Bespoke Vista acts as your processor under UK GDPR. This is what we put in place, and where each control stands today.

Contractual commitments

In our client contracts

  • Data Processing Agreement setting out UK GDPR responsibilities
  • International data-transfer safeguards: the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or UK Addendum where required, supported by a Transfer Risk Assessment
  • Confidentiality agreements for everyone working on your account
  • Intellectual-property assignment: deliverables belong to you
  • Data minimisation: we access only what the service needs
  • Incident-reporting obligations and timescales
  • Access removal and data return or deletion at the end of an engagement
Being implemented

Operational controls

  • Role-based access, granted in your own systems where possible
  • Multi-factor authentication on all accounts
  • Managed devices with endpoint protection
  • Activity logging
  • Joiner, mover and leaver procedures
  • Business-continuity arrangements for the Lahore office
Certification objectives

Independent assurance

Security controls are being developed with reference to Cyber Essentials and ISO/IEC 27001 requirements.

Bespoke Vista does not currently hold either certification, and we will say so plainly until we do.

FAQs

Straight answers.

Something else? Ask us directly.

Where is the work delivered?

Delivery takes place in our office in Lahore, Pakistan. Commercial ownership, service management and senior oversight remain in the UK with Bespoke Vista Ltd. Workshops and governance meetings can be led by our UK lead.

Who is accountable for delivery?

Bespoke Vista Ltd is contractually accountable for every service. Azeem Mushtaq, our founder, is the senior UK lead and your escalation point.

Will we communicate directly with the Lahore team?

Yes. Day-to-day work runs through a named delivery lead using channels you agree, typically Microsoft Teams and email. Scope, priorities, quality and escalation stay with your UK lead.

What UK working-hours overlap is available?

Our Lahore office normally works 09:00 to 18:00 Pakistan time. That gives a live overlap with UK mornings of about four hours in winter and five hours in summer. Later cover can be agreed for higher service levels.

How quickly can a delivery team be established?

A standard delivery pod can normally be mobilised within two to four weeks following contract signature, role confirmation, security approval and access provisioning. Specialist roles, dedicated recruitment, client security checks, complex systems or regulated data can extend this. We confirm a mobilisation plan before you commit.

Do you supply individual contractors?

No. We don't supply CVs, contractors or staff for you to manage. We deliver a defined service with agreed outputs, and we manage the people who deliver it.

How is confidential information protected?

Through a Data Processing Agreement, confidentiality agreements, role-based access, multi-factor authentication and managed devices, with international data-transfer safeguards where personal data is involved. The security section on this page shows the status of each control.

Can the team work in our Microsoft 365 environment?

Yes, and it is our preferred approach. Working in your tenant keeps information under your access controls, audit logs and retention policies.

Can our data remain within our systems?

In most cases, yes. We aim to work inside your systems so data is not copied to ours. Where that is not possible, we agree in writing what is transferred, how it is protected and when it is deleted.

What happens if a team member is absent?

Every recurring task has an SOP, so another trained team member can step in. Short absences are covered within the service. For single-analyst packages, how longer absences are covered is set out in your service definition.

Can capacity be increased or reduced?

Yes, through change control. Reductions take effect from the next billing month after notice. Increases depend on recruitment and onboarding lead times, which we confirm before you approve.

Are software and AI licences included?

No. Third-party software, AI tools and consumption charges are excluded. We tell you up front what each service needs, and you can license tools directly or have them recharged.

What is the minimum contract term?

Assessments, pilots and documentation projects are one-off, fixed-price engagements. Monthly managed services have a three-month initial term, paid monthly in advance, then continue on 30 days' notice.

What is included in the 30-day pilot?

One clearly defined process or workstream, a named delivery lead, agreed inputs and outputs, baseline measurements, weekly progress reviews, an end-of-pilot results report and a business case for ongoing delivery.

How is quality measured?

Against measures agreed in your service definition, typically accuracy, turnaround time, rework and service-level performance. Work is checked by a second person before release, sampled weekly and reported in the monthly service report.

What happens to access and data at contract end?

We remove all access, return or securely delete your data as set out in the Data Processing Agreement, and hand over all documentation and deliverables, which belong to you.

Which activities are out of scope?

Anything not in your service definition, including decisions on your behalf, accountability for delivering your projects, legal or financial advice, software development, third-party licences and on-site attendance unless agreed.

How are changes to scope handled?

Through change control. You raise a change request, we confirm the effect on capacity, timescale and price in writing, and nothing changes until you approve it.
