Privacy notice

How we use your information.

Last updated 21 November 2026.

Who we are

This website is operated by Bespoke Vista Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales ("Bespoke Vista", "we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal information described in this notice. You can contact us about privacy at hello@bespokevista.co.uk.

What we collect

  • Enquiries: when you use a form or email us, we receive your name, work email, and any organisation, phone number, organisation size, topic and message you provide.
  • Scorecards: your answers are processed in your browser and are not sent to us. If you request a full report, we receive your name, email, organisation and a summary of your answers and result.
  • Bookings: if you book through our booking page, Microsoft Bookings collects the details you enter on our behalf.
  • Technical data: we do not use analytics or tracking cookies on this site.

How we use it, and our lawful basis

  • To respond to your enquiry, send a report you requested and arrange a consultation: legitimate interests in responding to business enquiries, or steps prior to entering into a contract at your request.
  • To provide services if you become a client: performance of a contract.
  • To keep business records and meet legal obligations: legal obligation and legitimate interests.

We do not sell your information or use it for automated decision-making. We will only send you marketing emails if you ask us to, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

Who we share it with

  • Our email, document and booking providers (Microsoft 365, including Microsoft Bookings), which process data on our behalf.
  • Your own email provider, because our website forms currently open your email app to send your message.
  • Members of our team in our Lahore, Pakistan office who support enquiries and client work. This is an international transfer, protected by appropriate safeguards under UK data-protection law, such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or UK Addendum.
  • Professional advisers and authorities where required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information for up to 24 months after our last contact, unless you become a client, in which case we keep records for the duration of the contract and for six years afterwards for legal and accounting purposes.

Cookies and fonts

We do not set analytics or advertising cookies. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers and shares your IP address with Google for that purpose.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or erase your personal information, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to data portability. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time. To exercise these rights, email hello@bespokevista.co.uk. We will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. We would appreciate the chance to resolve your concern first.

Changes to this notice

We will update this notice when our practices change. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.

This notice covers our website and enquiries. Client personal data processed during an engagement is governed by the Data Processing Agreement in your contract.
