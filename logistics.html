Process simplification and cost reduction

Reduce operating cost by fixing complexity at its source

Much avoidable cost comes from complexity: repeated entry, unclear handoffs, rework and technology that isn't used. We find it, remove it and measure the difference.

  • Repeated manual entry
  • Duplicate systems
  • Unclear handoffs
  • Unnecessary approvals
  • Process delays
Full scope
  • Rework
  • Poor information quality
  • Weak ownership
  • Unused technology
  • Fragmented reporting
  • Activities that don't add enough value

Our method: map the current process; establish the baseline; identify delay, duplication and failure demand; simplify; assess automation; define the future process; build the business case; implement and measure. We measure cycle time, cost per transaction, manual touches, error and rework rates, service response time, system usage and staff capacity released. We don't promise a percentage saving before an assessment.

Why operational complexity matters

Transformation designed for operationally complex environments.

Logistics businesses operate through interconnected people, partners, processes, systems and data. Even a relatively small technology change can affect customer service, warehouse operations, transport planning, billing, reporting and compliance.

Bespoke Vista brings a business-and-technology perspective to these programmes. We help organisations understand dependencies, simplify processes, define the future operating model and coordinate delivery across operational and technical teams.

Typical situations

  • Replacing legacy operational systems
  • Automating order and exception workflows
  • Improving management reporting
  • Integrating customer and supplier information
  • Migrating collaboration and document platforms
  • Strengthening identity and access controls
  • Improving governance across programmes
  • Supporting organisational restructuring
  • Enabling distributed operational teams
  • Introducing AI-assisted knowledge processes

What we don't do

We are not a logistics operations consultancy. We don't provide route optimisation, warehouse layout engineering, fleet maintenance, customs brokerage, freight forwarding, demand forecasting, inventory planning, transport procurement or network design.

Where a programme needs that expertise, it comes from your operational teams or specialist partners. We make sure their decisions are reflected in the processes, systems and operating model we help you change.

Illustrative transformation scenario

Replacing a legacy operational system and automating exception handling

This scenario illustrates how the service may be applied and is not presented as an actual client engagement.

Client context
A UK distribution business with several sites, an ageing operational system and customer reporting compiled by hand.
Business problem
Support for the operational system ends within two years. Delivery exceptions are handled through email and spreadsheets, and there is no agreed future process.
Bespoke Vista's role
Transformation lead: discovery, operating-model design for exception management, migration governance, automation design and supplier coordination.
Complexity
Dependencies across customer service, operations, finance, IT and two technology suppliers, with cutover windows constrained by trading peaks.
Approach
Transformation Discovery, then process simplification, an automation and AI opportunity assessment, a migration plan with business-readiness activities, and change delivery leadership.
Deliverables
Current- and future-state process maps, an exception-management operating model, a workflow automation design with human approval points, a migration and cutover plan, RAID and governance packs, and a benefits framework.
Measured result
In a real engagement we would report cycle time, manual touches and error rate against the baseline. No results are shown because this is not a real engagement.
Lessons
Simplify the exception process before automating it; plan cutover around trading peaks; agree benefit measures before go-live.
FAQs

Straight answers.

Something else? Book a discovery call.

Are you a logistics operations consultancy?

No. We are a business and technology transformation consultancy that works with logistics and other operationally complex organisations. We don't advise on route optimisation, warehouse layout engineering, fleet maintenance, customs brokerage, freight forwarding, demand forecasting, inventory planning, transport procurement or network design.

What types of logistics transformation do you support?

Process automation, AI adoption, cybersecurity implementation, technology and data migration, target operating-model design, process simplification, complex programme delivery and remote or hybrid working. Typical situations include replacing legacy operational systems, automating exception workflows and improving management reporting.

Can you deliver the technical implementation?

We lead the business change, design, governance and delivery. Workflow automation in Microsoft 365 and Power Automate can be delivered directly where suitable. Specialist engineering, platform configuration and penetration testing are delivered by your internal teams or verified technical partners, depending on the engagement.

Do you work with internal IT teams and external suppliers?

Yes. Coordinating internal teams and suppliers, and translating between business and technical teams, is a core part of the role.

Can you lead a migration programme?

Yes: strategy, impact assessment, dependencies, cutover planning, business readiness, testing governance, communications, training, hypercare and decommissioning planning, working alongside your technical teams and partners.

How do you identify automation opportunities?

We map candidate processes, measure volumes, delays and manual touches, simplify the process first, then assess automation suitability, data and control risks, and indicative benefits.

Can you help us adopt Microsoft Copilot?

Yes: readiness and permissions review, governance and acceptable-use controls, use cases, user training and adoption measurement.

What is included in target operating-model design?

A current-state assessment, blueprint, capability map, process architecture, roles and responsibilities, governance model, technology implications, transition roadmap, implementation work packages and a benefits and KPI framework.

Can you recover a delayed or struggling programme?

Yes. We start with an independent review of scope, plan, governance, risks and stakeholder alignment, then agree a recovery plan and, if required, lead its delivery.

Do you provide cybersecurity testing?

No. We lead cybersecurity change programmes and control implementation. Penetration testing, security monitoring and managed detection are provided by specialist partners or your internal teams.

Can you support remote and hybrid working?

Yes, covering technology, ways of working, processes, information access, security, onboarding and adoption, not just collaboration tools.

How do you measure cost reduction?

We establish a baseline at the start, covering cycle time, cost per transaction, manual touches, error and rework rates and staff capacity, agree how progress will be evaluated, and report against it. We don't promise a percentage saving before an assessment.

Can you work on a fractional basis?

Yes. The Fractional Transformation Adviser runs from £1,950 to £4,500 a month, with two to four advisory sessions and written recommendations each month.

How quickly can an assessment begin?

A Transformation Discovery can usually begin within one to two weeks of agreement, subject to stakeholder availability and access.

What information is required to produce a proposal?

Your objectives, the scope and scale of the change (functions, sites, systems and users), key stakeholders, known constraints and timescales, and any existing plans or supplier arrangements. A discovery call is usually enough to produce an outline proposal.
