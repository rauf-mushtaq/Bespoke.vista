FAQs

Websites, apps and content questions.

Is the Member Portal a finished product?

The features listed are running today at EmptyHands. We set the portal up for each club with its own name, classes and fees. Features we haven't built yet aren't listed.

Why is EmptyHands your example?

EmptyHands is the karate and wellbeing school our founder has run in Ilford since 2006. It's our own school, which is why we can show it openly and test every change there first.

Who pays the card and PayPal fees?

You do, at the rates set by those providers. Payments go to your own account, not through us.

Can we move our existing website?

Usually, yes. We review it first and quote the move as part of the Business Website price.

Do you use AI to write content?

We use AI to help with drafting and ideas. A person edits every piece, and nothing is published without your approval.

What's the minimum term?

Member Portal and Content Creation: one month, then 30 days' notice. Business Website is a fixed-price project.

Can we buy online?

Where you see a Subscribe button, yes. Otherwise send a request and we'll confirm the set-up and send a payment link, usually within one working day.
