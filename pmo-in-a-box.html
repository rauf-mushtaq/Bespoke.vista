Clear boundaries

What PMO-in-a-Box isn't.

Being clear about limits is what keeps a managed PMO working well.

  • It doesn't replace your project managers or their accountability for delivery
  • It isn't unlimited: each package has a project limit and monthly capacity
  • It isn't software: we use your Microsoft 365 tools, or others by agreement
  • It doesn't make decisions for you: it makes sure the right decisions are visible on time

FAQs

PMO-in-a-Box questions.

All FAQs →

What do our project managers need to do?

Provide updates by an agreed day each week or month, using a short template we set up. We do the chasing, consolidation, RAID upkeep and reporting.

Which tools do you use?

Your Microsoft 365 environment by default: SharePoint, Teams, Excel and Planner, with Power BI where you hold licences. Smartsheet, Jira or similar by agreement.

Can you attend our governance meetings?

Your UK lead can attend governance meetings where agreed. The Lahore team prepares packs, logs and dashboards behind them.

What if we have more than ten projects?

The Transformation Office package covers multiple workstreams with a dedicated PMO analyst, from £7,500 a month, scoped in a proposal.

How do we start?

With a £495 Managed Operations Assessment, then a 30-day pilot covering a few projects, then a Foundation or Managed package with a three-month initial term.
