Straight answers

Questions UK buyers ask.

Anything else? Ask us directly.

Is this offshoring?

The work is delivered from our office in Lahore, Pakistan, and we're open about that. What you buy is a UK-managed service: a UK contract, a UK lead accountable for results, and agreed outputs and service levels. You don't manage anyone overseas.

Are you a staffing or virtual-assistant agency?

No. We don't send CVs or place individuals for you to manage. We agree the outcome, document the process, and run and quality-check the work ourselves.

Will this affect our UK team?

The service is designed to take repetitive and overflow work off your UK team so they can focus on customers, decisions and improvement, and to add capacity without permanent headcount.

How quickly can you start?

The Operations Assessment takes two weeks. A pilot team is typically working two to four weeks after that, because we start from documented processes and an existing office, not from a recruitment campaign.

Who will we deal with?

A named senior UK lead for scope, quality and service reviews, and a named team lead in Lahore for day-to-day coordination. Both are in your weekly reporting.

How is our data protected?

The team works inside your systems and Microsoft 365 tenant under your access controls. A data processing agreement and UK GDPR safeguards for international transfers are part of every contract, and team members sign confidentiality agreements.

What if the pilot doesn't work?

The pilot has success criteria agreed up front and a go / no-go review at day 60. If it isn't working, we stop, and you keep the SOPs and documentation produced.

Why not just use AI tools ourselves?

You should, where it makes sense. Tools on their own rarely change the work: someone has to redesign the process, maintain the automations and check the output. That's what the managed service provides.
