What we manage

Work that matters, but nobody has time to run.

A work package is a defined piece of work with an outcome, an owner on your side and an end date. Typical examples:

A system or supplier change

A new way of working introduced across teams, with owners, dates and evidence of completion.

A migration or cut-over plan

Clear boundaries

What Virtual Consultancy isn't.

Bigger or more technical work? Start with a discovery call and we'll suggest the right route, or the right partner.

  • It isn't a virtual-assistant or admin service: every plan is led by a senior consultant
  • It isn't staff supply: you don't manage our people, we deliver the agreed outputs
  • It doesn't take over your accountability: you own decisions, we make sure they're visible and acted on
  • It isn't unlimited: each plan has a work-package limit and monthly hours
  • It doesn't include specialist technical build, penetration testing or legal and financial advice

FAQs

Virtual Consultancy questions.

All FAQs →

What counts as a work package?

A defined piece of work with an outcome, an owner on your side and an end date, such as one phase of a system roll-out, a process change across teams or an audit action plan. If it's bigger than that, we'll suggest splitting it, or a Transformation Discovery.

Who will we work with?

Azeem Mushtaq, the senior UK consultant who leads the plan, attends key meetings and reviews every report, and a named coordinator in our Lahore office who keeps the logs, follows up actions and prepares reports.

Is it all online?

Yes. We work through Microsoft Teams, with documents in your SharePoint or a workspace we set up for you. Azeem works UK hours. Lahore office hours overlap with the UK morning, so updates are ready when your day starts.

What happens at the end of the trial?

You receive a status report and a recommendation. If you subscribe within 30 days, the £950 is credited in full against your first month. If not, there's nothing further to pay.

Can we change plan or cancel?

Yes. You can move up or down a plan from the next month. After the one-month minimum term, give 30 days' notice to cancel.

Do unused hours roll over?

No. Each plan includes a set amount of senior and coordinator time each month. Extra time can be agreed in advance at a quoted price.

How is this different from PMO-in-a-Box?

PMO-in-a-Box runs governance and reporting across a portfolio of projects your own project managers lead. Virtual Consultancy manages specific work packages for you, with senior consultant time included.

How do we pay?

The trial is invoiced in advance. Subscriptions are invoiced monthly in advance. No payment is taken on this website: we confirm scope with you first.

How do you protect our information?

Confidentiality agreements, access only to what the work needs, multi-factor authentication and no client data in public AI tools. See security and data protection.
