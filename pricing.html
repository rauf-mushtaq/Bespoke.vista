FAQs

Good questions.

Anything else? Just ask.

Do prices include VAT?

No. All prices are shown excluding VAT.

Is there a minimum term?

Subscriptions run month to month and can be cancelled with 30 days' notice. Managed services have a three-month minimum, then continue month to month.

How does annual billing work?

Pay annually for any subscription and get two months free: you pay for ten months and receive twelve.

Is this resource augmentation?

No. We agree outcomes, volumes and a service level, and we manage the team, tools and quality. You never have to manage individual staff.

How do you protect our data?

Our team works inside your systems and Microsoft 365 tenant under your access controls, covered by a data processing agreement and UK GDPR safeguards for international transfers. A UK lead is accountable for every service.

Can we start small?

Yes, and most clients do. Take a free scorecard, subscribe to Executive AI Intelligence, or start with a fixed-price assessment before committing to anything larger.
