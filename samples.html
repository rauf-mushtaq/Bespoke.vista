Standard operating procedure

An extract from an SOP in our controlled template. A standard SOP covers one process, up to five pages.

Illustrative example, not an actual client deliverable. Names, dates and figures are fictitious.

SOP IDTitleOwnerVersionReview date
SOP-GI-002Goods receipt and discrepancy handlingWarehouse Manager1.0 (approved)Annually

1. Purpose

To receive inbound goods accurately and resolve discrepancies within one working day.

2. Scope

All inbound deliveries at the main site. Excludes returns (see SOP-RT-001).

3. Procedure

  1. Check the advance shipping notice (ASN) against the purchase order.
  2. Count and inspect goods; record results in the warehouse system (control point C1).
  3. If quantities match, put away and confirm the location.
  4. If not, raise a discrepancy and notify the buyer within two hours.

4. Controls and KPIs

  • C1: receipt check recorded before put-away
  • KPI: dock-to-stock time; discrepancy resolution within one working day
