Our model

We don't sell people. We sell defined outcomes.

We don't supply CVs, contractors or staff for you to manage. Each service has defined deliverables, capacity and quality standards. A UK lead owns the result. AI-enabled processes take on repetitive steps, under human review.

  • Fixed monthly price for an agreed scope and capacity
  • Written service levels and regular reporting
  • One accountable UK contact
  • Documented SOPs, so the knowledge stays with you

Mobilisation

A standard delivery pod can normally be mobilised within two to four weeks following contract signature, role confirmation, security approval and access provisioning.

Mobilisation may take longer for specialist roles, dedicated recruitment, client security checks, complex systems, regulated data, or software and equipment provisioning. We confirm a mobilisation plan in writing before you commit.

Discover

Scorecard and assessment: the work, volumes, systems, constraints and data sensitivity.

Define

Scope, outputs, capacity, service levels and success measures, agreed in writing.

Mobilise

Access, onboarding, SOPs and baseline measures, following a written mobilisation plan.

Deliver

The team works to SOPs, with second-person checks and weekly progress reporting.

Review and improve

Monthly service review, KPI reporting, automation backlog and change control.

Security

Your data, your controls.
