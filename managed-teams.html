Our model

We don't sell people. We sell outcomes.

Traditional offshoring rents you people by the hour and leaves the managing to you. That's where quality slips and savings disappear.

Our managed teams work the other way round. Each service has defined deliverables, volumes and quality standards. A UK lead owns the result. AI and automation do the repetitive work, so costs fall over time rather than rise.

  • Fixed monthly price for an agreed scope
  • Service levels with real consequences
  • One accountable UK contact
  • Documented playbooks, so the knowledge stays with you
